# Kickoff
## Robot Design Strategy
- No robot designing
- Create a Robot WILL DO list
- Assign years to students to look back through FRC games to find common themes and usefull designs
- Parents are involved
- Everyone gives ideas (Bring new ideas and involve everyone to talk)
- NO idea is not crazy. Some the the best ideas are the most efficient (2481 Climb - 1706 Shoot and Move)
- Think Einstien robot
- Prototype, Prototype, Prototype

## Field Building
- Parents help to build the field
    * Get them involved and creates a better student expirence

## Kickoff Layout (Kickoff for 2 Days) - DO NOT DISCUSS ROBOT DESIGN
- Give students a notebook for ideas (No verbal discussion or sharing robot design)
1. Ice Breaker for Student and Parent Coffee Time
2. Parents Mentors and Students watch Kickoff video
3. Rule breakdown session for students and mentors | Parent Meeting
4. Lunch
5. Robot WILL DO list
6. Brainstorm Best Auto

## Day After Kickoff - BEGIN DISCUSSION ON ROBOT DESIGN
- Robot design
- Set team goal (Win Regionals/Divisions/Worlds)
